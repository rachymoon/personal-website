Research

Questions, methods, evidence.

Research projects live here even after they stop being current. Over time, this page can become a map of how one question led to another.

Current
Neural dynamics

Gamma oscillations and spiking activity during decision-making

Investigating how narrow-band gamma activity, gamma bursts, firing rates, and spike-phase relationships change around decisions and behavioral outcomes in rodent medial prefrontal cortex.

Current
Population coding

Decoding behavioral outcomes from neural population activity

Building time-resolved firing-rate representations and pseudo-populations to test when neural activity contains information about trial outcome.

Previous
Learning models

Learning, uncertainty, and confidence

Examining how model-derived learning variables relate to behavior and confidence, with an emphasis on interpretable statistical models and longitudinal change.

Research philosophy

I value analyses that remain close to the structure of the data while still asking a clear theoretical question. I am especially interested in combining descriptive visualization, statistical modeling, neural time-series analysis, and computational approaches.

Later, each project above can become its own permanent page with figures, methods, code links, publications, and archived notes.
